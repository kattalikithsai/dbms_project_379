Official Protocol

Four-Stage Redressal Architecture

Every citizen complaint is governed by statutory Service Level Agreements (SLAs) with complete audit trail.

01

Formal Registration

Citizen lodges details with location and photo attachment. System generates unique token GRV-2026-XXXX.

02

Officer Assignment

Assigned to competent Superintending Engineer or municipal officer with mandatory SLA response deadline.

03

Field Remediation

Ground crew dispatched for physical repairs, pipeline restoration, or clearance. Timestamped notes recorded in audit log.

04

Certified Resolution

Supervising officer certifies completion, resolution notes published to archive, and printable voucher issued.
